Hello and welcome to Lectura! Today we learn how to spot the machines on a network.
# Step 1: find your address
$ ip addr show
Warning: never scan a network without permission, it is strictly forbidden.
# Step 2: list the open ports
$ nmap -sV 192.168.1.10
This command lists the open ports: SSH, HTTP, DNS.
Oops, an error? Don't panic, it is easy to fix.
Well done, your first scan worked! Awesome, isn't it?
